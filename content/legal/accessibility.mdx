Domiutra wants this website to be usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice control, or keyboard-only navigation.

## Our target standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. These guidelines, published by the World Wide Web Consortium, describe how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities.

WCAG 2.2 Level AA is our target. We have not claimed full conformance, and we continue to test and improve the site.

## Measures we take

We have built accessibility into how the website is designed and developed:

- **Semantic HTML.** Pages use meaningful headings, landmarks, lists, and buttons so assistive technologies can interpret structure correctly.
- **Keyboard navigation.** Interactive elements, including navigation menus and forms, can be reached and operated with a keyboard. A skip link lets keyboard users bypass repeated navigation.
- **Visible focus.** Focused elements have a clear visible indicator.
- **Reduced motion.** Animations and transitions are reduced or removed when your device is set to prefer reduced motion.
- **Color contrast.** Text and interface elements are designed to meet WCAG contrast requirements on both the dark and light sections of the site.
- **Forms.** Form fields have associated labels, required fields are identified, and error messages explain what went wrong and how to fix it.
- **Text alternatives.** Meaningful images and icons have text alternatives, and decorative elements are hidden from assistive technologies.
- **Responsive layout.** Content adapts to different screen sizes and supports browser zoom.

## How we assess accessibility

We use a combination of methods:

- **Automated testing** with the axe accessibility engine to identify common issues during development.
- **Manual keyboard testing** of navigation, menus, and forms.
- **Screen reader spot checks** on key pages and flows.
- **Visual review** of contrast, focus indicators, zoom behavior, and reduced-motion settings.

Automated tools catch only some accessibility issues, so we treat manual review as an essential part of the process. We review accessibility when we make significant changes to the website.

## Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. Content from third-party services embedded in or linked from the website may not meet the same standards, and we cannot always control it. Older articles or documents may not have been reviewed to the same standard as newer pages.

We welcome your feedback. If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, or need information in an alternative format, please let us know. Helpful details include the page address, a description of the problem, and the browser and assistive technology you were using.
